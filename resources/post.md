# The post

Posted on Bluesky, September 2026, alongside a video recording of the demo:

> A little toy made to test #TypeSafe JEV. Visuals by Astra, logic by GLM 5.3. Each
> airplane only has information about itself and its surroundings — plus any nearby
> traffic — and all decisions are made by Jev, calibrated probabilities and all, in
> ~150 ms.

(Shared into the TypeSafe community Discord, where the reply thread kicked off the
"publish the repo" requests that this repository answers.)

## Follow-up lines kept in the pocket

- "Plane loses an engine → declares emergency → tower reserves the runway → two
  neighbors give way → 'Safe on the ground. Kettle on.' — no scripts, no state machine,
  just four questions and a decision model."
- The ask-the-question-well experiment: same model, same state, better-worded options —
  maintain 0.41 → 0.05. (Details in the root README, §1.)
